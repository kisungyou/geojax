Derivation
----------

The derivative was prepared from subjects S001 through S005 and unilateral
motor-imagery runs R04, R08, and R12. Event T1 denotes imagined left-fist
movement and T2 denotes imagined right-fist movement for these runs.

For every T1 or T2 annotation, the derivative stores the interval from
0.5 seconds through 3.5 seconds after event onset. The eight retained
sensorimotor channels, in file order, are:

Fc3., Fc4., C3.., C1.., C2.., C4.., Cp3., Cp4.

Signals are stored as float32 microvolts at the original 160 Hz sampling
rate. No filtering, covariance estimation, trial rejection, or learned
transformation was applied before storage. The executable tutorial performs
those steps visibly. The derivative contains 225 trials with shape
(225, 8, 480), plus event labels, subject identifiers, run identifiers,
channel names, and acquisition metadata.

The derivative contains no names or contact information. Subject identifiers
are the public pseudonymous identifiers used by the upstream dataset.
